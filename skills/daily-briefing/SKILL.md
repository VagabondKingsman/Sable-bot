---
name: daily-briefing
description: Morning briefing — today's date (UTC+7), what the user is tracking from memory, and top news on their topics.
tools: [memory_recall, web_search]
---

1. Start with today's date and weekday in Asia/Saigon (UTC+7).
2. memory_recall the user's ongoing projects, plans and interests (query: "dự án, kế hoạch, quan tâm").
3. For up to 3 interests, web_search today's most relevant news; one bullet each with a link.
4. List reminders or deadlines found in memory for today or this week.
5. Format: **Hôm nay** · **Đang theo dõi** · **Tin đáng chú ý** · **Việc cần nhớ**. Under 200 words.
6. If nothing new for a section, write "Không có gì mới" — do not invent items.
