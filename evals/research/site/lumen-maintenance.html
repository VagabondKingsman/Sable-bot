Lumen Bridge maintenance report 2025

Report date: 2025-09-30. Prepared by the Valdora Transport Office, structures unit.

The 2025 inspection of the Lumen Bridge found the main cables in good condition. Inspectors replaced 38 of the oak deck planks that showed signs of rot. The total cost of the 2025 maintenance programme was 1.2 million crowns, funded from the city's heritage budget.

The inspection team recommended repainting the steel frame before 2028. They also recommended a load limit of 600 people on the deck at any one time during festivals, because crowd loading during the annual Lantern Night festival exceeded design assumptions in 2024.

The office plans to install pedestrian counters with live data at both ends of the bridge in 2026. No closure of the bridge is planned for the 2026 works; work will be done at night in sections.

Contact: structures unit, Valdora Transport Office. This report replaces the 2023 maintenance summary.
