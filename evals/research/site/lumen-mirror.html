History of the Lumen Bridge

Published 2024-03-12 by the Valdora City Archive.

The Lumen Bridge is a pedestrian suspension bridge over the Kessel River in the fictional city of Valdora. Construction of the Lumen Bridge began in 1931 and the bridge opened to the public on 4 June 1934. The main span of the bridge is 412 metres long, which made it the longest pedestrian span in the region for nearly forty years.

The bridge was designed by the engineer Mira Castellan, who also designed the nearby Ostrel Viaduct. Castellan chose a suspension design because the river bed was too soft for intermediate piers. The deck is made of treated oak planks laid over a steel frame.

During a severe flood in 1962 the eastern anchorage was damaged and the bridge was closed for eleven months while engineers rebuilt the anchorage in reinforced concrete. The bridge reopened in 1963 with a new lighting system of 240 lamps, which gave the bridge its popular nickname "the string of pearls".

Today the bridge carries about 9,000 pedestrians on an average weekday, according to counts published by the city transport office. Cycling across the deck is not permitted; cyclists must dismount and walk.
